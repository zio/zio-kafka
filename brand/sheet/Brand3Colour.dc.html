Ink, paper and signal orange

The neutrals are the ZIO family's: ink, paper, snow, slate and mist. Signal orange is the Kafka colour, and it lives in one place in the logo: the hub at the centre of the Z. It is a surface and shape colour; orange 700 stands in when orange must be read as text, and orange 400 on dark grounds.

Tints and shades
Core signal orange is step 500 (outlined). Use 50 to 200 for fills and highlights, 400 on dark grounds and 700 whenever orange is text on light: links, code keywords, pressed states. Peach is step 200. The ink scale is the ZIO family's; step 900 is the core ink.

3.3
Light and dark mode
Signal orange is a light colour: 3.58:1 on white. It works for the hub, the tile, buttons and type of 24 px and up, but not for body text or links, which take orange 700. Labels on orange are always ink (5.09:1), never white. On dark the hub, links and buttons switch to orange 400.

Contrast pairs
Ratios are computed from the hex values (WCAG 2.x). Text needs 4.5:1, graphics and type of 24 px and up need 3:1. The rule that follows: orange is a surface and a shape colour; when orange has to be read as text on light, use orange 700. Anything written on orange is ink.

Pairings
Do ink rings, orange hub on paper or snow.
Do white rings and an orange hub on the ink tile.
Do snow rings and an orange 400 hub on ink.
Do the charcoal tile on dark grounds.
Read the guide to consumer tuning before you change poll settings.

Don't colour the whole Z; only the hub carries the accent.
Don't borrow a sibling's colour (here JSON raspberry) for the hub.
Don't put an ink hub on the ink tile; it disappears.
3.6
Proportion
Mostly paper and ink. Orange is about 6%: the hub, one button, a large headline word, the keywords in code. Peach is a trace, for highlights and tags. If orange fills a whole section it has stopped marking anything; the tile and the hero banner are the only large orange areas.

3.8
Next to ZIO Config
Signal orange sits close to ZIO Config's orange-red. That was a deliberate choice. Three things keep the two apart: Kafka's orange is yellower (hue 21° against Config's 14°), Kafka always pairs it with ink (an ink tile behind the mark, ink labels on buttons) rather than white, and the ring Z never appears on a Config page. When both libraries share a page, as on zio.dev, each keeps its own logo and the accent of the page belongs to neither.
