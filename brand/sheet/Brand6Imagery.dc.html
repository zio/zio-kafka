Brand sheet · 06 · Visual and imagery style

Records, lanes and a hub

Every picture in ZIO Kafka is built from the parts of the mark. A ring is a record, linked rings are a partition read left to right, one orange ring is the committed offset, and the bigger hub is the consumer. Flat, two colours and a grey, no photos.
6.1
Graphic language
PART 1
A record is a ring
The same ring as the mark: round, open, one stroke weight. Never filled.
PART 2
A partition is a lane
Rings linked into a row, read left to right. Order inside a lane is the order Kafka keeps.
PART 3
The offset is orange
One ring in orange marks the committed position. Done to its left in ink, still to come in grey.
PART 4
A consumer is the hub
Lanes meet in the bigger hub ring, the same hub as the Z. Only the hub and the offset take colour.
6.2
Pattern
Offset lanes of rings for covers, slide backgrounds and the hero. One ring in the field may take the accent; the rest sit one step off the ground colour.
6.3
Diagrams
Same parts as the mark. Rings for records, bars for order, the hub for a consumer. A reader who knows the logo can read the diagram.
Flow goes left to right. Partitions are horizontal lanes; time moves right. Assignments are 2 px dashed slate lines.
Labels in mono. Topic, partition, group and consumer names in JetBrains Mono 13 px, capitals only for the kind (TOPIC, GROUP).
Colour means state. Ink is processed, orange is the committed offset or the active consumer, grey is not yet read. Never colour for decoration.
Panels, not boxes. Groups and brokers are snow panels with a 1 px line, radius 14.
6.4
Code screenshots
Code in JetBrains Mono 15 px, 1.7 line height, on a snow or dark-code panel with 28 px padding. The snippet is the consumer half of the README example.
// consume, print, commit offsets in batches consumer .plainStream(Subscription.topics("random"), Serde.long, Serde.string) .tap(r => Console.printLine(r.value)) .map(_.offset) .aggregateAsyncWithin(Consumer.collectOffsets, Schedule.fixed(100.millis)) .mapZIO(_.commit) .runDrain

6.5
Icons
24 px grid, 2 px round stroke, drawn from rings and bars. The accent marks the one element the icon is about.
Producer
A record leaves to the right.
Consumer
Lanes meet in the hub.
Topic / partition
A lane of linked rings.
Commit offset
The orange ring is the position.
Consumer group
One topic, many consumers.
Rebalance
Plain line icon where no ring fits.
6.6
Rules
Do keep one colour accent: the offset or the hub.
Don't colour rings by partition or for variety.
Don't fill rings, or add gradients, glows or 3D.
Don't run lanes vertically or right to left.
