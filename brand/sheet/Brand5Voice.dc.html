Brand sheet · 05 · Voice and tone

Exact, calm, honest about delivery

ZIO Kafka turns Kafka topics into ZIO streams. Its readers run systems where a lost or doubled message costs money, so the voice is exact about guarantees, calm about rebalances and lag, and shows the code before it explains it.
5.1
Voice
Exact
Names the topic, the partition and the offset, the way Kafka and ZIO Kafka write them in logs.
Partition orders-3 at offset 1042
Calm under load
Rebalances, lag and slow consumers are normal in Kafka. Explain what happens and what to tune; never sound alarmed.
“ZIO Kafka pauses fetching a partition until your stream drains its buffer.”
Honest about guarantees
Delivery semantics are the first thing readers need to trust. State exactly what is guaranteed, and where it stops.
“At-least-once, unless you use transactions.”
Shows the code
A stream of records is easier to show than describe. Lead with the smallest runnable snippet, then explain it.
.map(_.offset) before the paragraph about offsets.
5.2
Tone by context
The voice stays the same. The tone moves along one slider as the reader's situation changes.
LOGS AND ERRORS
Short and precise
What failed, on which topic and partition, and what the library will do next.
RELEASE NOTES
Factual
What changed, who it affects, how to migrate. No adjectives.
DOCUMENTATION
Direct and patient
Code first, one idea per paragraph, every tuning knob with its trade-off.
SOCIAL AND TALKS
Friendly and brief
One idea, one link. A stream pun is fine; a benchmark without a link is not.
PRECISEFRIENDLY
5.3
Say this, not that
Kafka users get burned by vague promises about delivery. Every claim names its guarantee and its limit. These lines show tone; check each technical claim against the docs before publishing.
Delivery guarantees
Say “Commit after you process and you get at-least-once: a restart replays from the last committed offset.”
Not “Never lose a message.”
Exactly-once
Say “Transactions give exactly-once from topic to topic. Side effects outside Kafka still need to be idempotent.”
Not “Exactly-once everywhere, out of the box.”
Offsets
Say “Collect offsets with Consumer.collectOffsets and commit them in batches. Commit each record only at a few records per second.”
Not “Commit every record, just to be safe.”
Back-pressure
Say “If your stream falls behind, ZIO Kafka stops fetching a partition once its pre-fetch buffer is full, and resumes when your stream has drained it. Memory stays bounded per partition.”
Not “Handles any load automatically.”
Performance
Say “Often faster than using the Java client directly. See the benchmarks.”
Not “Blazing fast.”
5.4
In practice
Templates and sample lines; none of these are release claims.
DOCS
Use plainStream to read a topic as a stream of records. Map each record to its offset, then commit the offsets in batches.
RELEASE NOTE · TEMPLATE
Fixed: [what was wrong]. Affects: [who, which settings]. To migrate: [one line, or “nothing to do”].
SOCIAL
A Kafka topic is a stream. In ZIO Kafka it is a ZStream: map it, batch it, commit it.
TALK TITLE
Your topic is a stream: Kafka the ZIO way
5.5
Taglines
Proposals to choose from; the wording is for the maintainers to confirm. A tagline used as a headline carries no end punctuation.
1 · PROPOSED
Kafka streams, the ZIO way
Says what it is and whose style it follows. Used on the banner and cards in this sheet.
2 · OPTION
Type-safe Kafka for ZIO Streams
Leans on the integration with ZIO Streams, the library's main idea.
3 · OPTION
A Kafka client for ZIO
The first line of the README. Plain, and already in use.

5.7
Naming Apache Kafka
Write “Apache Kafka” on first mention in a page, then “Kafka”. Write our name as “ZIO Kafka”: never “Kafka ZIO”, “ZIO-Kafka” or “Zio Kafka”. Never show or adapt the Apache Kafka logo; the ring Z is our own drawing and only nods to it. Check the ASF trademark policy before shipping merchandise or a new logo file.
