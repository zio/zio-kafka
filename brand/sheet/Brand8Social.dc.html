8.5
Rules
Lockup, never tile + name. Cards use the full lockup. The tile is for avatars only and never sits beside “ZIO Kafka”.
Same grid everywhere. 80 px margins, lockup top left, tagline below, a short orange rule, the address bottom right. The margin is the safe area: X rounds the corners and lays its site label over the bottom left, so nothing important goes nearer an edge than 80 px.
Export at 2×. PNG for the platforms, with light and dark files: social-github-light.png, social-og-dark.png.
Release copy is a sample. The version and the three highlights are placeholders; take them from the release notes.
