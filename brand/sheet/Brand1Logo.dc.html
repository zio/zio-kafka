Brand sheet · 01 · Logo

Linked rings that draw a Z

Six rings and the links between them trace the Z of ZIO, and a bigger hub ring sits where the diagonal crosses the centre. The rings are records, the links are the flow, the hub is the consumer. On its ink tile it is the symbol; without the tile it is the Z of the wordmark. The ring-and-link idea is a loose nod to Apache Kafka, drawn as new geometry.
1.1
Symbol
Primary. Ink tile #101521, rings and links in white, hub in signal orange #E8590C. Orange appears only in the hub, the same as in the wordmark.
Dark grounds. Charcoal tile #2A3245, rings in white, hub in orange 400 #FF8A4C. For dark pages, dark mode and photos, where the ink tile would disappear.
1.2
Construction
Grid. 120 units square. Three columns and two rows of centres at 22, 60 and 98, 38 units apart (red dashes).
Rings. Six, radius 10.5 with a 10-unit stroke, so each is 31 units across at its outer edge.
Links. Same 10-unit stroke. Short bars join neighbours on the top and bottom rows, leaving a 7-unit gap at each ring. Two diagonals run at 45° from the top-right ring, through the hub, to the bottom-left ring: the Z (red line).
Hub. Radius 15 with an 11-unit stroke, 41 units across: about a third bigger than a ring (dashed circles). Always the accent colour: signal orange on light grounds and on the ink tile, orange 400 on dark grounds.
Tile. 120 square with a 26-unit corner radius (22%). The mark is scaled to 74% and centred (dashed box).

Wherever the full name appears, the bare mark is the Z of “ZIO”. It is sized to the capitals of JetBrains Mono (0.82 em, nudged down 0.045 em so the bottom rings sit on the baseline). “IO” follows in JetBrains Mono Medium, then a 0.28 em space and “Kafka” in Sora Semibold. Rings take the text colour; the hub takes orange on light and orange 400 on dark. The tile never sits beside a full “ZIO Kafka”, because that would say Z twice. In square spaces use the symbol alone. Horizontal (top) is the default. Stacked (bottom) puts “ZIO” over “Kafka”, both lines the same size and left-aligned, 0.14 em apart; use it for covers and square spaces where the horizontal logo would be too small.
