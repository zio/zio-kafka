The tile is the icon

The symbol already lives on a tile, so the app icon and the avatar are the same file. The ink tile stands out on a light tab, and on a dark tab the white rings and orange hub carry it, so the favicon keeps the tile too. The favicon is the same tile as the logo, scaled down.

The favicon is the logo tile itself, scaled down: six white rings, the links and the orange hub on the ink tile, with no simplified version. One drawing serves every size, so the tab, the bookmark, the app icon and the avatar all show the same mark. At 16 px the ring holes close up and the rings read as a dotted Z around an orange centre; from 32 px, the size on 2× screens, every ring is open. Ship favicon.svg and a favicon.ico with 16, 32 and 48, all from the symbol, plus a 180 px apple-touch-icon. Checked at 1× on light and dark tabs.
