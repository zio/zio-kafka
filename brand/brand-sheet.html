ZIO Kafka brand

The mark is the Z of ZIO drawn from linked rings, with a bigger hub ring at its centre in orange. The rings are records, the links are the flow, and the hub is the consumer they pass through. The Z sits in front of “IO” set in the code font and “Kafka” in Sora. Everything else in this sheet grows from that one construction.

The idea in three steps
STEP 1
Records are rings
Seven equal rings on a 3 × 3 grid, like records waiting in a topic. Their round shape is a loose nod to the rings of the Apache Kafka logo, drawn fresh.
STEP 2
Links draw the Z
Short bars join the top and bottom rows, and two diagonals run through the centre. The rings now trace the Z of ZIO.
STEP 3
The hub takes the colour
The centre ring grows into a hub, the consumer every record flows through, and takes orange. It is the only coloured part of the logo.

Ink and paper carry the page. Signal orange is the Kafka colour: the hub of the Z, the tile and one accent per layout. At 3.58:1 on white it is for shapes and large type only; text and links use orange 700 #C2410C (5.18:1). On dark grounds use orange 400 #FF8A4C.
